Designed coverage

Compliance coverage by design.

Rules are designed around the compliance requirements they cover. Argus runs those checks as your development workflow progresses and records the results as evidence of that coverage.

The compliance plan also defines requirements fulfilled through other processes, such as manual assessments. Those activities sit outside Argus’s scope.

Defined coverage

Specify the requirements and evidence each rule covers.

scope: the compliance plan
rules: defined checks
evidence: specified by each rule

Repeatable execution

Run the planned checks as your product evolves.

run: on configured pipeline events
record: results and check time
evidence: available with each report

How Argus works

Configure. Check. Record.

01

Configure

Select the rules for your planned compliance coverage. Add Argus to your pipeline and choose when it runs. Configure Jira or GitLab connectors to include evidence from those systems.

02

Check

Check whether files exist, match specified text patterns or meet configured age limits. Connector checks draw on issue and development workflow evidence from your connected systems.

03

Record

Keep the results as evidence of the checks performed. Read reports locally or submit them to Command Central, with the check time and Git commit when available.
